Before you use it

  • Celo Sepolia uses test money with no value. Once Celo mainnet launches, use --network mainnet only when you mean to pay real money.
  • A proof shows which approved build produced an answer, not that the answer is right. What a proof shows, and what it doesn't.
  • Payments on chain can't be reversed. Through buy, payment is taken before the service runs, so Attessa can't protect it.
  • The code is public at github.com/jesterkeri/attessa.
