Lookup tables: the commands, tools and options that are built or planned, marked where they aren't built yet, and refusal codes as the SDK and gateway return them.

CLI commands

Rows marked Coming are planned but not built yet. Rows marked Optional may not ship.

Buyer

CommandWhat it does
attessa initComing. Creates a wallet for this computer (key in the OS keychain), picks the network, sets spending limits. --network sepolia|mainnet.
attessa call <url>Coming. Checks the seller, gets the price, signs a payment permission, and pays only if the proof checks out. --body, --max-amount, --asset usdc|usat, --new, --no-wait, --dry-run (stops before signing), --json.
attessa call --via buy <url>Optional, not built yet. Buys through buy and records what came back. No payment protection. --accept-unprotected skips the prompt for agents.
attessa verify <requestId>Coming. Re-checks a past call against public Celo data, by request ID or settlement transaction. No wallet, no payment.
attessa cancel <requestId>Coming. Voids an unused payment permission on chain, for a little gas.
attessa statusComing. Spend today against your cap, pending permissions, recent verdicts. --pending, --agent <id>, or an origin.
attessa evidence exportComing. Exports your call records.
attessa release verifyOptional, not built yet. Rebuilds a release from the source you name and compares its measurements.

attessa selflinkComing. Links a Self Agent ID to your agent. Informational only.
attessa gateway pause|resume|status|exportComing. Stops and restarts new paid calls at your gateway, shows its state, and exports its records.

SDK: payAndVerify

OptionMeaning
maxAmountThe most this call may cost, in the asset's atomic units. The SDK uses the lower of this and your configured limit per call. Over it, nothing is signed: over_max_amount.
dailyCapSet in Limits. Today's settled plus reserved spend, per asset, per UTC day. Over it, nothing is signed: over_daily_cap.
assetWhich stablecoin to pay in: USDC, or USA₮ on mainnet.
requestIdYour own ID for this call: 32 random bytes, fresh for every new call. Never reuse one after its call has ended; the gateway answers request_id_conflict. To resume an interrupted call, send the same request again: the SDK finds it in its ledger and never pays twice.
newRequestForce a fresh request ID.

Refusal codes

A refusal is the product working: a named check failed, and the gateway shouldn't use your payment permission. If you had signed one, the SDK checks on Celo whether it was used, unless you passed --no-wait, and reports not_charged or settled_after_refusal. Codes marked R are refusals; the rest are conditions with a next step.

CodeWhat it meansNext step
proof_rejected RA check in the proof failed. The reason names it: response_hash_mismatch, request_mismatch, inputs_hash_mismatch, signer_not_active, release_not_approved, proof_expired and others.Wait for your permission to expire, or cancel it now.
payto_mismatch RThe service asked to be paid somewhere other than its registered address. It may be an impostor.Don't retry. Report it where you found the link.
origin_agent_mismatchThe service claims to be a different agent than the registry says.Don't retry. Report it.
over_max_amount RThe price is above your limit per call.Raise --max-amount if that's fine.
over_daily_cap RThis call would take today's spend past your cap.Wait until 00:00 UTC or raise --daily-cap.
endpoint_not_registeredThe origin isn't an attested endpoint.Ask the seller to register. Optional, not built yet: pay without protection with --via buy.
endpoint_retiredThe owner stopped selling at this origin. Nothing was signed.Coming: attessa status --agent <id> lists current endpoints.
liveness_failedThe sealed machine didn't answer a fresh check.Retry later. Coming: attessa status <origin> shows when it last answered.
payment_invalidThe payment permission was refused, for example for insufficient funds.Add funds to your address.
authorization_expiring RYour permission expires in under 10 s, too close to settle safely.Retry; the SDK signs a fresh 120 s window.
authorization_window_too_shortYour permission expires too soon for this service, usually a slow clock or network.Call again.
execution_timeoutThe service didn't respond in 20 s.Retry with a new request.
enclave_unavailableThe service is offline.Retry later.
gateway_pausedThe service can't take payments right now.Retry later.
gateway_busyThe service is busy, so the call was refused before it ran. If you had signed a payment permission, the SDK checks on Celo whether it was used, unless you passed --no-wait, and reports not_charged or settled_after_refusal.Try again shortly.
rate_limitedToo many requests from you, or too many of your paid calls to this service ended without settling in the last hour. The call was refused before it ran. If you had signed a payment permission, the SDK checks on Celo whether it was used, unless you passed --no-wait, and reports not_charged or settled_after_refusal.Try again after the time the service gives.
settle_pendingPayment sent and confirming, usually under 5 s.None: the CLI keeps checking.
settle_failedThe payment didn't go through, so the answer was withheld.Retry with a new request.
uncertainThe connection dropped after you signed. Attessa checks Celo for up to 2 minutes.None: it resolves to paid or not charged.
not_chargedConfirmed on Celo: your permission expired unused.None: done.
paid_but_rejectedYou paid, but the answer didn't check out. On-chain payments can't be reversed.Report the gateway to the marketplace, with your record.
settled_after_refusalThe service refused, but your permission was used anyway. The gateway is at fault.Report the gateway, with your record.
already_usedToo late to cancel: the payment went through.None: Attessa fetches your answer.
payment_reused, request_id_conflictThis request ID or permission was already used for a different call.Call again with --new.
in_progressThis call is already running.None: it checks again in 2 s.
result_not_foundNo stored result for this request ID. Results expire after 24 hours.Coming: attessa status --pending.
bad_requestThe service can't read this input.Fix the input and call again.
quote_unavailable, quote_malformedThe service didn't ask for payment in a form Attessa can read.Check the URL, or update the CLI.
network_mismatchYour RPC is on a different chain than the registry.Set --rpc for the right network.
rpc_unavailableCan't reach Celo. Before signing, the call stops and nothing is signed. While checking a paid call, the call stays uncertain until Celo can be read; an RPC error never fails a check.Retry or set another --rpc.
max_amount_requiredNo limit per call is set, so nothing was signed.Coming: run attessa init or pass --max-amount.
no_walletNo wallet set up yet.Coming: run attessa init.
attestation_stale RSeller: the machine's report is over 30 minutes old.attessa signer register --fresh.
release_not_approved RSeller: the machine runs a build the agent hasn't approved.Approve it, or run the approved build.
debug_pcrs RSeller: the machine is in debug mode.Restart without --debug-mode.
binding_mismatch RSeller: the report was made for another agent, chain or origin.Re-attest with the right config.
cert_expiredSeller: the report's certificates have expired.attessa signer register --fresh.
not_owner RSeller: only the agent's owner can do this.Run it from the owner wallet.
not_operator RSeller: this address isn't the operator for the origin.Run it from the operator wallet.
origin_taken RSeller: the origin is registered to another agent.Use another origin, or ask that owner to retire it.
invalid_origin RSeller: the origin isn't just a scheme and host.Use just the scheme and host, like https://risk.example (no path, lowercase).
invalid_pcr RSeller: the build measurements file can't be read. It should be the JSON that nitro-cli build-enclave prints.Pass the file nitro-cli wrote.
release_exists RSeller: this build is already approved for the agent.None. Coming: attessa status shows it.
endpoint_inactive RSeller: the origin isn't an active endpoint for the agent.Add it with attessa endpoint add.
signer_not_active, signer_mismatch, release_mismatch RSeller: attessa signer renew refused, for example because the signer already expired or the report is from a different key.Expired: restart with attessa enclave start, then attessa signer register. Otherwise: run attessa signer renew on the machine that holds this key.
self_link_invalid RSeller: the Self Agent ID must be held by this wallet (Self's linked mode) and have a current human proof.Coming: check it in the Self app, then run attessa selflink again.
nsm_unavailableSeller: the machine couldn't get a signed report from AWS.Use an instance with enclaves enabled.
