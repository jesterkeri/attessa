What this site does today

  • No accounts. There's no sign-in, and Attessa sets no cookies.
  • Your network choice (Celo Sepolia or Celo mainnet) is saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only.
  • Fonts and scripts load from Google Fonts, cdnjs, jsDelivr and unpkg, so those services see your IP address when a page loads.
  • The proof explorer reads public Celo data, and the gateway's stored result for a request ID you paste. It needs no wallet.

When you make a call

Calls go from your own computer to the service you choose, never through this site. The service's gateway can read the request body you send. Your record of each call stays on your computer, in ~/.attessa/evidence.jsonl.
