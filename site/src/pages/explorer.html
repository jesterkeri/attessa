Attessa lets an agent pay a service only when the answer is signed by the code the seller approved. Paste a call's settlement transaction, and the explorer checks its on-chain part from public Celo data: the payment, the endpoint registered to the address it paid, and that endpoint's approved builds and signers, though not which of them answered. No wallet, no payment. Planned: looking a call up by its request ID and checking its proof, which needs a public copy of the proof that nothing serves yet.
